Access to good counselling should not depend on where you live. Whether you are in Nanaimo, Courtenay, Campbell River, Prince George, or anywhere else in British Columbia — online counselling removes the geographic barrier and brings qualified support directly to you.

I am Dave Dyck, a Canadian Certified Counsellor (MC, CCC) based in Nanaimo, BC. I offer secure, confidential virtual counselling sessions to individuals, couples, and families across British Columbia — including fully covered ICBC sessions for motor vehicle accident recovery.

What Online Counselling Looks Like

Virtual sessions work exactly like in-person sessions — just through a secure video platform. You receive a link before your appointment, join from a private space on your phone, tablet, or computer, and we work together in real time. No commute. No waiting room. No special software required.

For many people, the convenience of online counselling actually improves consistency — making it easier to keep appointments, especially during periods of high stress or when driving feels difficult after an accident.

Online ICBC Counselling Across BC

If you were in a motor vehicle accident in BC, your ICBC-funded counselling sessions are fully covered virtually. This is especially important for people living in smaller communities where specialized MVA counselling is hard to access locally.

  • 12 pre-approved sessions within the first 12 weeks — no referral needed
  • Direct billing to ICBC — no upfront cost in most cases
  • Virtual sessions fully covered anywhere in BC
  • Family members may also qualify — see ICBC Family Counselling
  • Extensions available if you need ongoing support

See ICBC Counselling Nanaimo for full details on eligibility and how to start.

What Online Counselling Addresses

Virtual sessions are effective for the same range of issues as in-person counselling. Areas I work with online include:

  • Trauma and PTSD — including accident-related trauma and EMI therapy adapted for virtual delivery
  • Anxiety and nervous system overwhelm — anxiety counselling for panic, worry, and chronic stress
  • ICBC motor vehicle accident recovery — driving anxiety, emotional numbness, hypervigilance
  • Men's mental health — men's counselling for burnout, shutdown, and relationship strain
  • Couples and relationship counselling — virtual couples sessions for communication and reconnection
  • Family counselling — family support including post-accident recovery
  • Concussion emotional recovery — concussion counselling for irritability, anxiety, and overwhelm

Who Online Counselling Is Right For

Virtual counselling works especially well for:

  • People in smaller communities across Vancouver Island and BC without local access to specialized counselling
  • ICBC clients whose driving anxiety makes in-person appointments difficult
  • Professionals with busy or unpredictable schedules
  • People who value the privacy of connecting from home
  • Couples where one or both partners work remotely or travel frequently
  • Anyone who wants consistent, ongoing support without logistical barriers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is online counselling as effective as in-person counselling?
Yes. Research consistently shows that online counselling produces outcomes equivalent to in-person therapy for anxiety, depression, trauma, and relationship difficulties. For many clients, the convenience and privacy of virtual sessions actually improves engagement and consistency.
Is online ICBC counselling covered in BC?
Yes. ICBC's Enhanced Care program fully covers virtual counselling sessions anywhere in British Columbia. If you were in a motor vehicle accident in BC, you are likely eligible for funded online counselling through direct billing — no referral required within 12 weeks.
What platform do you use for online counselling?
Sessions are conducted through a secure, confidential video platform. You will receive a simple link before your session. No special software is required — just a private space and a stable internet connection.
Do I need to live in Nanaimo to access counselling?
No. I offer virtual counselling to anyone across British Columbia, including all of Vancouver Island, the Lower Mainland, the Interior, and Northern BC. My focus is on Vancouver Island communities, but virtual sessions remove geographic barriers entirely.
What issues can be addressed through online counselling?
Online counselling is effective for trauma and PTSD, anxiety and panic, ICBC recovery after car accidents, men's mental health, couples and relationship difficulties, family stress, concussion-related emotional changes, burnout, and emotional overwhelm.
How do I get started with online counselling in BC?
Book a free 15-minute consultation. We can discuss what you are navigating, confirm ICBC eligibility if applicable, and schedule your first session — often within the week. No referral required.
Is online counselling private and confidential?
Yes. All sessions are conducted through a secure, encrypted platform in compliance with BC privacy legislation. All counselling records are kept strictly confidential in accordance with the CCPA Code of Ethics and BC PIPA.
Can couples do online counselling together?
Yes. Virtual couples counselling works well when both partners are in a private space, either together or in separate locations. Many couples find virtual sessions easier to schedule than in-person appointments.
