## STEP 2 — Link these into your visible site navigation

These pages exist and are internally cross-linked to each other and to your
existing pages, but nothing on your *visible* site currently points to them
yet — they're accessible by direct URL but not discoverable through normal
browsing. To avoid orphan pages (the standing rule we've followed
throughout this project), add links to these from wherever makes sense:

- Your blog/articles index page, if you have one
- Relevant existing articles (e.g. link to the new PTSD article from your
  general trauma pages)
- Consider adding 2–3 of these to a "related reading" section on your
  ICBC service pages
